## What I do

At **Crazy Clearance** (DropThePriceUK Limited, trading as Crazy Clearance) I run the social channels and the online store:

- **Social media:** Instagram, Facebook and TikTok for the Tipton, Oldham and Aylesbury branches, **450K+ followers**, with weekly stock-drop Reels, TikToks and Stories
- **Shopify:** **600+ product lines** on [crazyclearance.uk](https://crazyclearance.uk): prices, RRP, descriptions, images, stock, and 3D product models made with Meshy AI, Claude and Canva
- **AI video ads:** a 10-step workflow that turns one product listing into a 30-second ad. Real products and real prices, no made-up claims
- **Reply bot:** an AI agent that answers Instagram and Facebook DMs from staff-approved answers only
- **Reserve stock idea:** my proposal to grow reach without paying for ads, which got me promoted from customer service

I'm also doing an **MSc in Business Analytics** at The University of Law, and I run **Copy The Facts Telugu** on YouTube (**1.2M+ views**).
